Boot straight to the NYC acceptance example →

Pre-fills a cart with the $45 hoodie and the $20 book, pins the shipping address to New York 10001, picks the $10 flat rate, and lands on checkout. Expected: hoodie exempt (NY Clothing < $110 rule), book $1.78, shipping $0.27, total tax $2.05 — versus $0.89 shipping tax on a one-class store.

What's set up

  • A New York store in USD, taxes on, taxed by the customer's shipping address
  • Products: Hoodie $45, Winter coat $150, Jacket $60, a $20 book, Mug $10, Blender $40, plus a $200 Designer coat (MA excess test) and a $300 Luxury coat (RI excess test)
  • Product categories Clothing and Books mapped to tax categories
  • Example rules: NY clothing under $110 at Zero rate (cliff), MA clothing with the first $175 per item exempt (excess), RI clothing with the first $250 exempt (excess), books zero-rated in the UK, shipping rules for NY, AZ, CA, HI, MN, IL, BE and the UK
  • Example tax rates for those places, and a $10 flat rate to the US and UK
  • Every new field is outlined with a New badge; hover it for what it does, or use Take the tour on each changed screen

Where to look

/wctc-suite.phpFull validation suite (80+ cases across opt-out parity, resolution, rules, shipping, order editing, Store API, Analytics, merchant setup mistakes, excess-only limits and the tax-free report). Append ?format=json for machine-readable output.

Products → CategoriesTax category on each product category
Shop → checkoutNYC order: hoodie + book shows $0.27 shipping tax. Ship a $200 or $300 Clothing coat to MA or RI to see excess-only tax on just the amount above the limit.
