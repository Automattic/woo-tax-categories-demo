EU / UK / AU store — what's set up

  • Berlin store in EUR, taxes on, taxed by shipping address (OSS: destination rates), one zone with a €4.90 flat rate
  • Products: Paperback €20 (Books), Children's jumper €30 (Kids), Coffee beans €12 (Pantry), E-book €10 (virtual, Digital books), Mug €10 (own class), Rain jacket €60 (own class)
  • Four categories (Books, Children's clothing, Food, Digital books) with country-level rules that leave gaps on purpose — the jumper is Standard in Germany and Zero in Ireland without touching the product
  • One shipping rule: Belgium uses "whole charge at the lowest rate in the box". Everywhere else — including Australia — has no rule, which is the main claim: turn it on and shipping is right with no configuration
  • Rates for DE, FR, IT, IE, BE, NL (Standard + Reduced), GB (VAT only), AU (GST only). Illustrative, not advice
