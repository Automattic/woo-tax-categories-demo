Tax Categories Playground

A throwaway WordPress test store with WooCommerce and the tax categories prototype, ready to try in your browser. Nothing is saved anywhere; close the tab and it's gone.

Open the test store

Boots in about 30 seconds · blueprint from bright-willow.view.fast

What's set up

  • A store with WooCommerce active, this plugin on, taxes calculated by the customer's shipping address
  • A handful of products across different categories (books, clothing, food, a digital download, a kitchen item) at a mix of price points, so the category rules have something to resolve
  • Tax categories and country-level rules that leave gaps on purpose: a children's item is Standard in one country and Zero in another with no change to the product; a paperback is reduced-rated in some, zero-rated in others
  • One shipping rule for a jurisdiction that treats a mixed basket differently; everywhere else has no rule, which is the main claim — turn it on and shipping is right with no configuration
  • Illustrative tax rate rows for every country the store sells to. Not advice

Where to look

/wctc-eu-check.phpEight real carts calculated end-to-end against expected numbers (destinations across the EU, UK and Australia, plus an opt-out parity check)
WooCommerce → Settings → Tax → Tax categoriesCategories, rules and shipping rules (the store opens here)
WooCommerce → Tax-free salesDate-ranged report of sales that paid no tax because of a rule, bucketed by tax category
Products → CategoriesTax category on each product category (shows the inheritance)
Shop → checkoutAdd two items to a cart and see shipping tax split across their classes, instead of the single-class behaviour stock WooCommerce falls back to
